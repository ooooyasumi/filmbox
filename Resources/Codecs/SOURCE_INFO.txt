The archive was obtained directly from FFmpeg over HTTPS. The SHA256 above is
pinned in scripts/build-codec-runtime.sh and checked before source execution.
It records archive integrity; a SHA256 alone is not an upstream PGP signature
verification. No third-party precompiled FFmpeg binary is redistributed.

No FFmpeg source changes. The unmodified corresponding source archive and
extracted source are retained under .build/CodecRuntime/downloads and src.
The app also contains the exact archive and build script/configuration under
Contents/Resources/Licenses/Codecs, alongside the license texts.
The bundled build-codec-runtime.sh contains all configure/build arguments;
build-configuration.txt and runtime-info.json describe the actual binary build.
The FFmpeg libraries are statically linked only into the independent ffmpeg
and ffprobe executables; Filmbox communicates with these tools as subprocesses.
No GPL, GPLv3-only or nonfree build components are enabled. No x264/x265 or
other third-party codec library is linked. Dynamic dependencies are restricted
to the operating system's Apple frameworks and /usr/lib libraries.
